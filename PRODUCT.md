# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People exploring Vedic astrology for self-reflection, and people comparing two saved birth profiles. They need clear chart results, accessible explanations, and control over the birth information they save.

## Product Purpose

CHANDRA calculates exploratory Vedic birth charts, helps people inspect placements and lunar phases, and offers a reflective comparison of two charts. The interface must distinguish symbolic interpretation from scientific prediction or certified Jyotish calculations.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, mysterious, simple. The experience should feel contemplative and human while keeping practical tasks direct.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered dashboards and decorative UI that competes with the chart.
- Neon sci-fi effects, purple gradients, and generic horoscope clichés.
- Compatibility scores presented as a verdict about a relationship.
- Hidden keyboard behavior, low-contrast text, or motion that ignores user preferences.

## Design Principles

- Let the chart and the person’s question lead each view.
- Reveal detail through clear, familiar controls instead of extra decoration.
- Keep astrology curious and reflective, with honest limits on what calculations mean.
- Preserve the user’s control of personal birth details and existing workflows.
- Support keyboard use, readable contrast, and reduced motion throughout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target was specified. Preserve semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, chart keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support. Do not depend on color alone to communicate state.
