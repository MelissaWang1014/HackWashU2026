How are these charts calculated?

Planetary positions use Astronomy Engine with an approximate Lahiri sidereal offset, mean lunar nodes, and whole-sign houses. D9 and Vimshottari periods are derived from those positions. Results can differ from professional ephemerides, especially near boundaries. This is an exploratory calculator, not a certified Jyotish engine.

Why is birth time required?

Enter your local birth time to calculate your rising sign, houses, and timing periods.

What does compatibility mean here?

Compare two charts, Moon signs, and angular relationships. These are prompts for understanding one another, not a prediction of relationship success or a traditional 36-point marriage assessment.
